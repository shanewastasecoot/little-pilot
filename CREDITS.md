Changes: the biplane's propeller is cut out of the body mesh at load time so it can spin (`scripts/aircraft/prop_splitter.gd`); the biplane and the jet (red recoloured to the chosen paint) are repainted by a hue-swap shader for paint jobs (`scripts/aircraft/model_recolor.gd`) and are rescaled and reoriented in their model scenes. The mermaid's sample backdrop, bubbles and animation are removed, two skinning faults are repaired, her tail is hue-shifted per mermaid in a shader, and her poses (sitting, waving, diving) are new bone animations made in code. House roofs (including the Kenney houses' teal roofs) are recoloured at load time for variety; the three balloons are split out of their pack. The wind turbine's blades are regrouped around the hub at load time so they can spin. The townspeople are KayKit Adventurers characters dressed like the player's outfits, simplified to about half their triangles, with their walk, idle and cheer animations baked into vertex animation textures for the city crowd (`tools/bake_kaykit_people.gd`, `kk_*_crowd.res` / `kk_*_vat.res`).

## Made by this project

- **Her house**: inside and outside built in code from the same floor plan, `resources/buildings/her_house.json` (inside: `scripts/buildings/building_generator.gd`; outside walls, windows, door, porch and roofs: `scripts/buildings/house_exterior.gd`; the fenced garden, swings, trampoline and sandpit: `scripts/buildings/house_garden.gd`). The stairs, railings, toy box, toys, ball, bubbles, trophy shelf, light switches, the paint easel, quilt chest, TVs and their cartoon, meal trays, the hidden star and the bouncy sofa and beds are built in code too.
- **The other insides** (the post offices, the city's houses, the hat shop): built in code from floor plans (`resources/buildings/post_office.json`, `scripts/buildings/city_house_layouts.gd`, `scripts/buildings/hat_shop_layout.gd`) with the KayKit furniture above; the counters, pigeonholes, conveyor belt, scales, hat stands, try-on mirror and signs are built in code.
- **3D art:** island and islet terrain, the ocean shader (`shaders/ocean.gdshader`), runway, the arched hangar (`scripts/world/landmarks/hangar.gd`), tower cranes, city streets and fountain, windsock, fields and stars are built from Godot primitives and generated meshes. Distant trees use generated cones.
- **Player character outfit props** (pilot cap and scarf, princess gown and tiara, astronaut helmet and pack, superhero cape and star, dino hood and tail, the smile), **delivery props** (the parcel, letter, mail bag, balloon, plushies, meals) and **the hats** (straw hat, sun hat, flat cap, beanie, postie cap, chef's hat, ranger hat, beret...): built in Blender by `tools/blender/build_props.py` (`assets/characters/props/props.glb`; see `docs/characters.md`); the toon shaders are in `shaders/`. The hat shop's menu pictures (`assets/ui/hats/`) are renders of those props (`tools/hat_icons.tscn`).
- **Sound effects and music:** synthesised at startup by `scripts/core/sfx.gd` and `scripts/core/music.gd`.
- **Font:** Godot's built-in default font.
- **Effects:** sparkles, splashes, dust, the startled birds, the under-the-sea moment, fireworks and the dragon's fire are drawn in code.
- **Files made by the project's own tools** (no third-party licence): `assets/characters/props/props.glb`, `assets/ui/hats/`.
